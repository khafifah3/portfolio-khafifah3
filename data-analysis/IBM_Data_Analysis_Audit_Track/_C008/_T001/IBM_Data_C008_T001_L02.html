What I'm Doing¶

4. Extracting and Plotting a Single Country (Haiti)¶

With the data in shape, I can start creating line plots. Let's begin with a case study on Haiti.

In 2010, Haiti was hit by a catastrophic magnitude 7.0 earthquake, devastating the country and affecting roughly three million people. Canada responded by stepping up refugee acceptance — and the immigration data should reflect that.

Step 1: Extract the series for Haiti.

haiti = df_can.loc['Haiti', years]
haiti.head()

1980    1666
1981    3692
1982    3498
1983    2860
1984    1418
Name: Haiti, dtype: int64

Result: Haiti is now a pandas Series with years as the index and immigrant counts as the values.

Step 2: Plot the line.

haiti.plot(kind='line')

plt.title('Immigration from Haiti')
plt.ylabel('Number of immigrants')
plt.xlabel('Years')

plt.show(); #adding semicolon (;) to supress "<Axes: >" text

Result: The spike after 2010 is unmistakable. Let's make it even clearer by annotating the earthquake event.

# Convert index from string to int for better text positioning
haiti.index = haiti.index.map(int)

haiti.plot(kind='line')

plt.title('Immigration from Haiti')
plt.ylabel('Number of Immigrants')
plt.xlabel('Years')

# Annotate the 2010 Earthquake
plt.text(2000, 6000, '2010 Earthquake') # (x-pos, y-pos, "Text")

plt.show()

Result: a text annotation "2010 Earthquake" placed at the year 2000, pointing toward the post-2010 surge.

df_CI = df_can.loc[['India', 'China'], years]

Step 2: Transpose and plot.

Because df_CI has countries as rows and years as columns, I need to transpose it so that years become the x-axis and each country becomes a separate line.

df_CI = df_CI.transpose()
df_CI.head()
India China
1980 8880 5123
1981 8670 6682
1982 8147 3308
1983 7338 1863
1984 5704 1527

Now plot:

df_CI.index = df_CI.index.map(int)  # convert to integers for clean x-axis

df_CI.plot(kind='line')

plt.title('Immigrants from China and India')
plt.ylabel('Number of Immigrants')
plt.xlabel('Years')

plt.show()

Result: China and India have very similar immigration trends through the years.

Why didn't we need to transpose Haiti's data?
Because haiti was a Series (one-dimensional), with years already in its index. A DataFrame with multiple columns (India and China) needs transposing so each column becomes a separate plotted line.

Line plot is a handy tool to display several dependent variables against one independent variable. However, it is recommended that no more than 5-10 lines on a single graph; any more than that and it becomes difficult to interpret.
